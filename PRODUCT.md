# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers book services, providers manage appointments and availability, and admins oversee the platform. The app must support fast everyday booking workflows on mobile and desktop without making any role feel secondary.

## Product Purpose

This is a multi-role booking platform for discovery, scheduling, appointment management, and operational oversight. Success means users can complete their primary task quickly, understand status at a glance, and trust the system to behave consistently across roles.

## Brand Personality

Premium, modern, and efficient. The interface should feel polished, direct, and trustworthy, with enough warmth to feel human but not decorative or slow.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards, cluttered card stacks, beige-brass luxury defaults, overly playful UI, low-contrast gray-on-gray layouts, and mobile-heavy interfaces that hide core actions.

## Design Principles

1. Make the primary workflow obvious on every screen.
2. Use status, hierarchy, and spacing to reduce decision friction.
3. Keep the system role-aware without fragmenting the experience.
4. Favor clarity and responsiveness over decorative effects.
5. Preserve trust with readable contrast, predictable behavior, and strong error states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard support, clear focus states, and usable layouts on small screens. Support reduced motion, avoid relying on color alone for meaning, and keep form and status text readable in every role.
